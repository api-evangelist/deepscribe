Dr. Douglas Flora, an oncologist, cancer survivor, and author, discusses where AI is delivering value in oncology, why clinical trust begins in back-office solutions, and why AI must understand the whole patient context. He identifies ambient documentation, pattern recognition in radiology/pathology, and emerging agentic intelligence as the three areas where AI proves valuable.
