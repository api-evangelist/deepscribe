A podcast episode featuring Dr. Aparna Parikh discussing systemic barriers to cancer care access that exist before patients reach the clinic, and exploring where AI can meaningfully assist versus where structural solutions are needed. She warns that without deliberate design, AI tools could widen disparities by benefiting already-resourced populations most.
